# Exploration Lenses

Deep exploration requires multiple lenses to see the full picture.

## Problem Lens

**Focus**: Understanding the problem deeply

Questions:
- What is the user's mental model of the problem?
- What are the symptoms vs root causes?
- What would happen if we do nothing?
- What are the dependencies of this problem?

## User Lens

**Focus**: Who benefits and how

Questions:
- Who experiences the pain directly?
- Who pays for the solution?
- Who decides to adopt?
- What is the user's workflow before/after?

## Technical Lens

**Focus**: What is technically feasible

Questions:
- What technologies are available?
- What is the complexity ceiling?
- What are the integration points?
- What are the performance requirements?

## Business Lens

**Focus**: Value and constraints

Questions:
- What is the ROI?
- What is the time-to-value?
- What are the competitive implications?
- What are the regulatory concerns?

## Operational Lens

**Focus**: Long-term sustainability

Questions:
- How will this be maintained?
- What is the operational cost?
- How will incidents be handled?
- What is the rollback strategy?

## Exploration Checklist

```
- [ ] Problem lens completed
- [ ] User lens completed
- [ ] Technical lens completed
- [ ] Business lens completed
- [ ] Operational lens completed
- [ ] Synthesis document written
- [ ] Adversarial review triggered
```
